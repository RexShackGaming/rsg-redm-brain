# vorp_imapviewtool


A developer tool to easily find, view, and toggle IPLs (Interior Proxy Lists) for RedM vorp_core. **⚠️ WARNING: DO NOT USE THIS SCRIPT ON LIVE SERVERS - DEVELOPMENT USE ONLY** - **Visual IPL Detection**: Automatically detects nearby IPLs and draws lines to their locations

Source: https://github.com/VORPCORE/vorp_imapviewtool

## Commands (RegisterCommand)

**client:** `/iplDistanceView`, `/removeAll`, `/requestAll`, `/testIpl`, `/toggleIplView`
