# rsg-npcs

Version 2.0.5. rsg-npcs

**Dynamic NPC spawner and ambient sound system for RedM using RSG Core.** > Spawns NPCs dynamically based on player distance, plays local or remote audio, and supports optional blips and scenarios. > Designed for RSG Core servers to bring life to towns and custom map areas.

Source: https://github.com/Rexshack-RedM/rsg-npcs

**Dependencies:** `rsg-core`, `xsound`
